MOZLYT website - how to put it online

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html, shop.html, career.html, what-we-do.html, about.html, blog.html
  img/  (your logo and photo)
Keep all of them together in the same folder. Do not rename them.

STEPS
1. Buy a basic web hosting plan (any plan that gives you a File Manager).
2. Connect your domain mozlyt.com to the new hosting. Your hosting company has a
   "connect an existing domain" guide. Your domain is currently with Wix, so you
   will change its name servers (or DNS records) to the ones your new host gives you.
   Do this last, because your old Wix site goes offline when you switch.
3. Open the File Manager, go into the folder called public_html, and upload
   everything in this folder (the six pages and the img folder).
   index.html must sit directly inside public_html.
4. Turn on the free SSL certificate (HTTPS) in the hosting panel.
5. Visit mozlyt.com on your phone and test: menu, Book on WhatsApp, Add to list.

THINGS TO CHECK BEFORE YOU GO LIVE
- Your Instagram, TikTok and Facebook links (they point to /mozlyt).
- The email Info@mozlyt.com exists (create it in the hosting panel) or change it to mozlyt0@gmail.com.
- The story and founder text on the About page.
- Add Privacy and Terms pages later.
