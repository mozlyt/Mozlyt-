How Payments Work on MOZLYT: Cards, Mobile Money and Paying Your Franchise

By MOZLYT · How MOZLYT works · 15 October 2026

Money questions come first for most people, so here is how payments work. The short version: you always see the amount in your own currency before you pay, and you always get a record of the payment.

Prices in your own currency

Each franchise sets prices in its own country's currency, so someone in Nigeria sees naira, someone in Kenya sees shillings and someone in Ghana sees cedis. There is no hidden exchange rate. The number you see is the number you pay.

Two ways to pay

  • Online payment: in countries where it is switched on, you pay by card or Mobile Money on a secure checkout. Your payment is confirmed automatically.
  • Manual payment: in other countries, you pay using the Mobile Money, bank or cash details your franchise gives you. Then you send the payment reference in your dashboard, and your franchise confirms it.

Why not online everywhere?

Online checkout needs a payment provider that supports the country and its currency. MOZLYT switches it on country by country as providers confirm support. Until then manual payment works in every country, and it keeps the process safe because every payment has a reference.

Staying safe

  • Only pay to the details shown in your MOZLYT dashboard or given by your official franchise.
  • Always keep your payment reference and screenshot.
  • MOZLYT staff will never ask for your PIN or card number on WhatsApp.
  • If something looks wrong, message MOZLYT on WhatsApp before you pay.

What happens after you pay

Your payment shows as waiting until it is confirmed, then as confirmed. When it is confirmed your plan or lessons open. If a payment cannot be matched, you can contact us with your reference and we sort it out.
