Take a shop

We train you, supply you, send you customers and give you the tools to run your own online store.

Your own online store

Open your store with its own link. Choose your name, banner, products and prices. Premium can use its own custom address, like www.yourbrand.com.

Products at wholesale prices

Quality-checked products supplied by MOZLYT. You set your own selling price and keep the difference.

A shop dashboard

See your orders, your products, what you pay MOZLYT and your selling price in one place.

Room to run your own ads

Advertise your store your own way. Plus and Premium can connect Facebook, Instagram and TikTok tracking to see what brings customers.

Customer jobs sent to you

When a customer books a service near you, MOZLYT sends you the job. You earn a commission on each one.

Training included

Every shop comes with the business lessons. Premium also includes one skill of your choice.

7 days free, cancel any time
