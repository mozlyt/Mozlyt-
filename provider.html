MOZLYT service provider terms
  1. I am qualified for every service I choose, and the ID and certificate I send are mine and true.
  2. I will follow the rules, quality standards and prices of MOZLYT, and I will not do anything that stands against them.
  3. I will respect every client, arrive on time, and keep their information and safety private and secure.
  4. I will promote the beliefs and mission of MOZLYT and I will not harm its name.
  5. MOZLYT may check my documents, and may pause or end my access if I break these terms or am not qualified for a service.
  6. MOZLYT keeps my documents private and uses them only to verify me. The arrangement for each booking is agreed with MOZLYT before I start.
