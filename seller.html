MOZLYT Franchise

A franchise is your own MOZLYT branch, like a partner location. It is not as easy as opening a shop: you must agree to the MOZLYT franchise terms and send your ID card and manager certificate for checking. Once approved, your first 7 days are free. After that it is GH₵1,000 a month.

  • Your own dashboard, under MOZLYT
  • Recruit sellers and trainees with your own link
  • Their payments come to you, not to MOZLYT
  • Set the prices your recruits pay and sell at
  • Pause or activate your recruits if payment is late
  • The full shop, every lesson and every skill
  • Best wholesale prices from MOZLYT
  • You pay MOZLYT only the agreed amount, monthly or yearly
Step 1: create your account. Step 2: log in, agree to the terms and upload your documents. Step 3: MOZLYT checks them and opens your franchise login. Nothing is charged now.

Start with a free trial. After that, keep your store open with a monthly or yearly plan.

By signing up you agree to sell honestly and deliver what customers order.

Franchise application

A franchise is not like opening a shop. Before you get franchise access, MOZLYT must check who you are and that you are qualified to run a branch. If your certificate reads management or marketing, your franchise dashboard opens at once with 7 days free. Any other certificate is checked by MOZLYT within about 2 working days.

MOZLYT franchise terms
  1. I will follow the rules, policies and prices of MOZLYT, and I will not do anything that stands against them.
  2. I will promote the beliefs and mission of MOZLYT, and I will not harm its name.
  3. Everything I submit is true. The ID and certificate are mine.
  4. MOZLYT may check my documents, and may pause or end my franchise if I break these terms.
  5. My first 7 days are free after approval. After that I pay the franchise fee (GH₵1,000 a month) on time, or my branch is paused.
  6. MOZLYT keeps my documents private and uses them only to verify me. I understand they are read by an automated AI checker, and a person at MOZLYT may also review them.
