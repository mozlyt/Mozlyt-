HOW TO MANAGE YOUR SHOP PRODUCTS FROM A GOOGLE SHEET

ONE-TIME SETUP (about 10 minutes)
1. Open Google Sheets and create a new sheet. Choose File > Import > Upload and pick
   mozlyt-products-template.csv. Choose "Replace spreadsheet". Name the tab "Products".
2. Choose File > Share > Publish to web.
   In the first box choose the "Products" tab, in the second box choose
   "Comma-separated values (.csv)", then press Publish.
3. Copy the link it gives you. It looks like https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/.../pub?output=csv
4. Send me that link and I will put it into the shop.
   (Or open shop.html and paste it between the quotes in: var SHEET_CSV_URL="";)

THE COLUMNS (keep the first row exactly as it is)
  category  Use one of: Massage tools and recovery, Fitness equipment, Nutrition,
            Skincare and beauty, Cooking tools, Fashion, Gift packages.
            A new name creates a new section automatically.
  name      The product name.
  price     A number such as 250 shows as GH₵ 250. Leave empty to show "Price on request".
  status    in stock, sold out, or hidden (hidden removes it from the shop).
  photo     Optional. A link to a picture of the product. Empty shows an icon.

EVERY DAY AFTER THAT
  Edit the sheet on your phone. The shop updates by itself, usually within a few minutes.
  If the sheet cannot be reached, the shop shows its built-in list instead.
