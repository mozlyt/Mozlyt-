We started with wellness. Now we help people build a living from it.

MOZLYT is a wellness, fitness and beauty brand in Accra. We bring trusted services to customers, and give sellers the training, products and tools to earn on their own terms.

Our story

MOZLYT began in Accra as a wellness and fitness business, offering massage and body care to people who needed it.

Along the way we saw two things. Customers want services they can trust, brought to them. And many people want to earn, but do not have the training, the stock or the shop to start.

So we built MOZLYT to do both. We serve customers directly, and we train, supply and support sellers who go on to run their own online stores.

Our mission

Make trusted wellness services easy to book, and make selling them a real career.

Our vision

A network of independent sellers across Ghana, each running their own business under one trusted brand.

What we believe

Trust comes first

Customers should know who is coming, what it costs and what to expect.

Freedom through ownership

People do their best work when the business is their own.

Quality you can count on

We train our sellers and check the products we supply.

We grow together

When our sellers earn well, MOZLYT grows. We win together.
