Become a dispatch rider

Deliver MOZLYT orders in your area. Log in, or create an account with your ID, selfie, licence and your delivery machine number. Your franchise approves you, then sends deliveries to you.

Apply to be a dispatch rider

Nothing is charged to apply. Your franchise checks your documents and replies within about 2 working days.

MOZLYT dispatch rider terms
  1. The documents, selfie and machine number I send are mine and true.
  2. I will deliver every order safely, on time and untouched, and I will respect every customer.
  3. I agree to share my live GPS location with my franchise while I am online or carrying a delivery, so it can follow my delivery.
  4. I will follow the rules of MOZLYT and my franchise and keep customer information private.
  5. My franchise may pause or end my access if I break these terms.
